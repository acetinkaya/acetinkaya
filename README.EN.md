## 👋 Hello

For over eight years I have been researching and teaching in artificial intelligence, autonomous systems and embedded technologies. My focus is turning theoretical models into working engineering solutions — from fuzzy logic and machine learning to embedded control systems, computer vision and robotics.

I release almost everything I produce as open source: lecture notes, code examples, book materials and workshop content. I ranked **3rd in 2025** and **5th in 2024** in Turkey's GitHub open-source contribution rankings.

Alongside my academic work I serve as an instructor, advisor and principal investigator on projects supported by TÜBİTAK, ISTKA and TEKNOFEST. **15 patents** from the work I have supervised have been registered internationally, and **2 have been commercialized** and transferred to industrial companies.
